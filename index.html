Gate test

This version checks that the camera can see the car cross each gate. No times are saved yet.

  1. Mount the phone sideways so it sees both rocks, and keep it still.
  2. Turn the volume up and the ringer switch on.
  3. Tap Start camera, then mark the gates.

Turn the phone sideways for a bigger picture. If the picture turns but the app does not, swipe down from the top-right corner and turn off Portrait Orientation Lock.
